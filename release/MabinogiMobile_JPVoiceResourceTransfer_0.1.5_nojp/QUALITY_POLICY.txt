Quality policy for the full-rebuild release

The production tool copies the original JP FSB5 payload into a rebuilt UnityFS
resource stream. It does not force the payload into the original TW resource
slot and does not lower the sample rate to fit.

For every changed bundle the tool updates AudioClip resource offsets and sizes,
rebuilds the UnityFS bundle, rewrites the custom .blob cumulative end table,
and verifies the result with UnityPy and complete FSB5 decoding. The bundle
ordinal and count remain unchanged, so AssetPackages/index remains usable.

Structural, format, metadata, source mutation, and write-back errors are hard
failures. The tool does not silently skip a selected target. A successful
patch-report.json has transaction_status=committed and replacement_mode=
full-rebuild.

The official 59826-obd-manifest is deliberately not modified. Launcher repair,
integrity rejection, map loading, online checks, and anti-cheat behavior remain
client/service policy and are not guaranteed by this offline tool.
